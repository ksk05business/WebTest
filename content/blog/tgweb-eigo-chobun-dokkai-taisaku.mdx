## 【実際の練習問題】段落ごとに読み進めながら設問を解く

実際の練習問題を使って、段落ごとに読み進めながら設問に答えていく流れを見てみましょう。次のような4段落構成の長文に、5つの設問が続きます。

### 第1段落を読み、対応する設問1に答える

> For centuries, merchants traveling between East Asia and the Mediterranean relied on a network of trade routes that historians now refer to collectively as the Silk Road. These routes were never a single fixed path but rather a shifting web of roads that changed according to political conditions, available water sources, and the safety of mountain passes. Many of the maps used by merchants during this period survive only in fragments, having been damaged by age, poor storage, or the environments in which they were kept.

**設問1：Why do many trade route maps from this period survive only in fragments?**

- They were damaged by age, poor storage, or their environments.
- They were never completed by the original mapmakers.
- They were destroyed deliberately by rival trading nations.
- They were intentionally cut apart by merchants for security reasons.

第1段落の最後の一文に「damaged by age, poor storage, or the environments」とそのまま書かれているため、正解は1つ目の選択肢です。段落を読み終えた直後にこの設問を解けば、本文を探し直す必要がありません。

### 第2段落を読み、対応する設問2に答える

> Dr. Helena Voss, a historian specializing in medieval cartography, has spent much of her career reconstructing damaged trade route maps using fragments held in museum archives across several countries. Her method involves comparing handwriting styles, ink composition, and geographic details across multiple partial maps to determine which fragments likely belonged to the same original document. When Voss began this work in 2011, only a small number of fragments had been successfully matched to their original sources.

**設問2：Dr. Helena Voss's work involves**

- translating merchant records into modern languages.
- reconstructing damaged maps by comparing fragments from archives.
- drawing entirely new maps of ancient trade routes.
- building physical models of ancient trading cities.

第2段落の1文目「reconstructing damaged trade route maps using fragments held in museum archives」がそのまま言い換えられている2つ目の選択肢が正解です。

### 第3段落を読み、対応する設問3に答える

> In 2017, a graduate student working with Voss, Tobias Lindgren, developed a digital comparison technique that analyzed subtle variations in paper fiber patterns visible under specialized lighting, allowing fragments from the same original sheet to be matched far more reliably than through visual inspection alone. This method significantly accelerated the reconstruction process and led to the successful matching of dozens of previously unconnected fragments.

**設問3：Tobias Lindgren's technique helped by**

- translating handwriting from unknown historical languages.
- analyzing paper fiber patterns to match fragments more reliably.
- creating entirely new maps without using original fragments.
- destroying damaged fragments that could not be matched.

第3段落「analyzed subtle variations in paper fiber patterns」と一致する2つ目の選択肢が正解です。

### 第4段落を読み、対応する設問4に答える

> Voss's team has since reconstructed several route maps that provide new insight into how merchants adjusted their paths during periods of regional conflict. She is now working with a museum consortium to digitize the completed reconstructions, making them freely available to researchers worldwide. Voss hopes that continued fragment matching, supported by Lindgren's technique, will eventually allow historians to reconstruct a much fuller picture of how trade routes evolved over several centuries.

**設問4：What is Voss currently working on?**

- Physically restoring the original historical trade routes.
- Ending all further fragment-matching research.
- Digitizing completed map reconstructions for public research access.
- Removing digitized maps from public access.

第4段落「working with a museum consortium to digitize the completed reconstructions, making them freely available」と一致する3つ目の選択肢が正解です。

<PointBox>
  設問1〜4は、本文の段落が出てくる順番とほぼ対応しています。段落を1つ読み終えるたびに、対応する設問をその場で解いてしまえば、あとで本文全体を読み返す手間がなくなります。
</PointBox>

## 最後の設問だけは「全体を見渡す」特殊タイプ

**設問5：Which of the following statements is true?**

- The Silk Road was a single, permanently fixed path.
- Lindgren's technique relied only on visual inspection of handwriting.
- Voss's reconstructions have not revealed anything new about merchant routes.
- Few fragments had been successfully matched before Voss's work began in 2011.

正解は4つ目の選択肢（第2段落の「only a small number of fragments had been successfully matched」に一致）です。この設問だけは、4つの選択肢それぞれが本文の別々の段落に対応しているため、段落ごとに読み進める方式では即答できません。

<PointBox label="最後の設問は「消去法」で解く">
  「Which of the following statements is true?」のような全体判定タイプの設問は、必ず長文の最後（各段落を読み終えたあと）に解きましょう。すでに段落ごとに内容を把握できているので、あとは各選択肢を本文と照合する消去法で解けます。また、誤りの選択肢には「a single, permanently fixed path（唯一の固定された道）」「relied only on（〜だけに頼った）」のような、本文よりも言い切りが強すぎる断定的な表現が使われがちです。このような極端な言葉を含む選択肢は、疑ってかかるとミスを減らせます。
</PointBox>

## よくある質問

### Q. TG-WEB英語の長文読解は、どのくらいの時間配分で解けばいいですか？

TG-WEB英語は10問/15分で、1つの長文に対して5問が紐づくグループ出題形式です。つまり実質「2つの長文を15分で読んで答える」ことになるため、1つの長文につき使える時間はおよそ7〜8分（読解＋設問5問）が目安です。1問あたり1.5分という数字だけで焦らず、長文単位で時間配分を考えましょう。

### Q. 設問は英文のどこから探せばいいですか？

多くの場合、設問は本文の段落が出てくる順番とおおむね対応しています。1問目は序盤の段落、2問目はその次の段落…というように、段落を1つ読むごとに対応する設問に答えていくと、本文を何度も読み返さずに済みます。ただし、最後の設問（「次のうち正しいものはどれか」のような全体判定タイプ）だけは、本文全体を通して確認する必要がある特殊なタイプです。

### Q. 選択肢を選ぶときに気をつけるポイントはありますか？
